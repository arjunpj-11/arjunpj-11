## 🧭 About Me

> *“Mathematics taught me to enjoy difficult problems. Programming gave me a way to turn that instinct into real systems. This is only the beginning.”*

Mathematics shaped how I think: understand the problem, break it into smaller parts, test assumptions, and keep improving the solution. Programming gave me a way to turn that thinking into systems and products people can actually use.

I enjoy learning how things work—from software architecture and cloud infrastructure to intelligent and distributed systems. Every skill I develop becomes a foundation for the next stage of my journey.

Full-stack engineering is where I started, not where I intend to stop. I want to keep evolving, explore unfamiliar fields, and apply what I learn to increasingly meaningful problems.

---

## ⚙️ How I Work

- **Understand first:** I study the problem before proposing a solution.
- **Break down complexity:** I turn difficult problems into clear, manageable parts.
- **Design with boundaries:** I build modular systems with explicit responsibilities.
- **Engineer for reality:** I treat security, testing, deployment, and maintainability as part of development—not as afterthoughts.
- **Learn through feedback:** I improve through experimentation, code reviews, and real product constraints.
- **Own the complete journey:** I work from the initial idea through production delivery.

> **My operating principle: Research. Understand. Improve.**
